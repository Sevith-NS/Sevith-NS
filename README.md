### 🚀 Things I've built

#### ✦ Polaris — India + US markets, one honest view
> An invite-only quant desk and portfolio manager. Every suggestion explains itself, factor by factor.

- **Broker sync** — read-only holdings from 8 brokers next to paper portfolios
- **Quant trade plans** — multi-factor votes, ATR/swing stops, 1.5R / 3R targets, half-Kelly sizing
- **Risk** — VaR & CVaR in currency, Sharpe, Sortino, drawdown, beta, Markowitz max-Sharpe
- **AI trading desk** — analyst agents → bull/bear debate → risk committee → PM sign-off, streamed over SSE and scored on realized alpha
- **Backtester** — event-driven, 13 strategies (incl. a Pine v6 port), out-of-sample, walk-forward and Monte Carlo
